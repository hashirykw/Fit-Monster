What are the timings?

Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday and Saturday: 6:30 AM to 4 AM. Wednesday and Sunday: 6:30 AM to 1 AM. The open/closed status on this site updates live.

How much is membership?

We don't hide it, we just don't put it on a poster. It depends on the plan and on coaching. Message the desk on WhatsApp and you'll get today's rate in one reply.

Do women train here?

Yes, every day, on the full floor, with female coaches on it. Ask the desk which hours suit you best.

Can I see the gym before joining?

Please do. Message the desk, pick a time, and walk the floor before you decide.
