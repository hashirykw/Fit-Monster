Training

Train like it costs something.

Four programmes, one floor, coaches on it from 6:30 AM until the lights go off. Here is what each one actually involves.

Ask about a programme

Programmes

Pick your work.

Every membership gets the floor and the coaches on it. Personal training adds a coach who is only yours.

Strength & hypertrophy

The main event. Structured blocks on free weights and machines, with the load going up only when the form has earned it.

  • Coaches on the floor during every session
  • Split routines built for muscle, not just sweat
  • Hammer Strength dumbbells and Nautilus stacks
  • Progress checked, not guessed

Personal training

One coach, one plan, built around your body, your schedule and what you actually want to look like in four months.

  • Assessment before the first set
  • Programme written for you, adjusted every week
  • Diet guidance alongside the training
  • Book through the front desk

Women's training

Women train across the full floor with female coaches on it. Strength, shape and conditioning, never a watered-down version.

  • Female coaches on the floor
  • Glutes, back and full-body programmes
  • Machine and free-weight coaching
  • Ask the desk about the hours that suit you

Conditioning

The engine behind the muscle. Turf, rig and cardio work for fat loss, stamina and the days you want to leave soaked.

  • Turf lane under the red rig
  • Ropes, kettlebells, med balls, plyo boxes
  • Treadmills and bikes
  • Finishers that fit after any split

From our feed · The Back Series

Five rows to a wider back.

Filmed on our floor with our coaches. Each clip loops while it's on screen.

01 · Lats

Close-grip lat pulldown

Builds width across your upper back as you drive the elbows down toward your ribs.

02 · Lats

Straight-arm pulldown

Keeping the arm straight takes the biceps out, so your lat does all the pulling.

03 · Rhomboids & mid traps

Seated row machine

A fixed, supported position keeps your torso still so the upper-middle back does the work.

04 · Mid back & lats

Seated cable row

Squeeze your shoulder blades together to strengthen the middle back and support better posture.

05 · Lats & mid back

Bent-over row · Smith

The guided bar path lets you row with control and build overall back thickness.
